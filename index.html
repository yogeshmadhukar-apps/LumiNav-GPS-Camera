4. Camera Permission & GPS Camera Usage

LumiNav requests access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) solely for the following functional purposes:

  • Displaying the live camera viewfinder within the GPS Camera module.
  • Capturing photos with embedded GPS coordinates, elevation, compass heading, timestamp, and address overlays.
  • Operating the physical camera LED flash/torch when using the Flashlight utility.

Privacy Guarantee: Camera frames and captured images are processed strictly in real-time on your device screen. We do not record video silently in the background, nor do we upload captured photos to any cloud storage, external AI servers, or remote endpoints.

5. Location / GPS Data Usage

LumiNav requests access to Fine Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) and Coarse Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) permissions to provide core features:

  • GPS Camera: To obtain real-time latitude, longitude, altitude/elevation, speed, bearing, and accuracy radius for photo stamp overlays.
  • Compass Module: To calculate magnetic declination and determine True North versus Magnetic North.
  • Reverse Geocoding: To display approximate street address, city, state, or postal information locally on your camera overlay using standard Android system services.

Local Processing: Location data is accessed only while the app is actively in use or when capturing a photo. Location telemetry is never logged in the background when the app is closed, nor is your precise location sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

6. Sensor Telemetry (Compass, Gyroscope & Accelerometer)

LumiNav accesses device hardware sensors, including the magnetometer (magnetic field sensor), accelerometer, and gyroscope. This hardware telemetry is used strictly to:

  • Calculate real-time magnetic heading and direction for the 3D Compass.
  • Compute dual-axis pitch and roll angles for the Bubble Level utility.
  • Detect device shake gestures to toggle the flashlight (if enabled by the user in Settings).

Sensor telemetry remains entirely inside device RAM and is discarded continuously as new readings arrive.

7. Photos, Media Storage & EXIF Metadata

To save captured photos to your device gallery, LumiNav utilizes standard Android storage mechanisms (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on legacy OS versions or standard MediaStore API on modern Android versions).

  • Captured photos are saved directly into your device's MediaStore or Gallery under user control.
  • When enabled, GPS metadata (latitude, longitude, altitude, timestamp) may be written into the photo's EXIF metadata tags or stamped visually onto the image.
  • Sharing Responsibility: Photos remain stored locally on your physical device. If you choose to share, export, or upload a photo via messaging apps or social networks, you retain full ownership and responsibility for any attached location metadata contained in that image.

8. Location & Sensor Accuracy Disclaimer

All location coordinates, elevation readings, compass directions, addresses, and sensor measurements provided by LumiNav are calculated from your device hardware and standard Android APIs. Readings may contain variances or inaccuracies due to:

  • Weak or degraded GPS satellite signals.
  • Indoor usage, underground structures, tunnels, or dense urban building canyons.
  • Environmental and atmospheric weather obstructions.
  • Magnetic interference from nearby electrical lines, metal objects, vehicle chassis, or magnetic phone mounts.
  • Variance or calibration limits inherent in individual Android device hardware.
  • Outdated or unavailable system geocoding address databases.

CRITICAL NOTICE: LumiNav GPS Camera is designed for general informational, educational, and amateur photography purposes only. LumiNav must NEVER be presented or relied upon as a certified instrument for professional surveying, land navigation, emergency response, aviation, maritime navigation, military operations, or safety-critical tasks. Always independently verify critical location and direction information with certified equipment.

9. GPS Camera Limitations & User Responsibility

While LumiNav strives to provide prompt and clear location overlays on captured images, we do not guarantee that every photo captured will contain 100% accurate or uninterrupted location telemetry. Factors such as cold GPS satellite acquisition times, device sleep modes, or permission revoking can cause temporary location gaps.

Users are solely responsible for reviewing stamped location metadata on photos before utilizing them for legal, commercial, or official documentation purposes.
